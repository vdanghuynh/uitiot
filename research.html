How the areas fit together

A digital twin needs edge computing to run close to the physical system. Edge and cloud orchestration needs wireless links that meet latency and reliability targets. Quantum methods and learning-based defenses then address the hardest optimization and security questions across all three. Projects in the group usually touch two or more areas.

Platforms and applications

The group grew out of work on wireless sensor networks, IoT reconfiguration, and smart cities, and that practical base still shapes how we test new methods.

Smart cities and IoT

Projects with VINIF and VNUHCM combine IoT, 5G, big data, and AI for urban services. The SmartCity Station senses and predicts air quality, and the UiTiOt testbeds let us reprogram and evaluate large IoT networks.

Edge AI, UAVs, and robots

Our new edge boards, cameras, flight controllers, and robots let us validate digital twins and learning agents on hardware for UAV networks and physical AI.
