Join us

We welcome motivated students and collaborators who want to turn optimization and learning methods into systems that work on real networks.

Students at UIT

Undergraduate and master's students at UIT can join through research and thesis projects. The People page lists current topics, from federated learning at the edge to digital twins for UAV networks and quantum reinforcement learning. If a topic fits your interests, or you have your own idea in one of our four areas, write to us.

PhD applicants and visiting researchers

We are looking for PhD candidates in digital twins, edge intelligence, next-generation wireless networks, and quantum machine learning and optimization. Visiting researchers and project collaborators are also welcome.

What to send

  • A short CV and your transcript.
  • One paragraph on the problem you want to work on and why.
  • Links to code, reports, or papers you have written, if any.

Strong foundations in at least one of optimization, machine learning, networking, or programming help you start quickly. Curiosity and persistence matter more than a long list of tools.
